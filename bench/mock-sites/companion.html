F-08 · OPTIONAL CURSOR COMPANION

Cursor companion gallery.

A page with ordinary controls for checking that the companion can be drawn over it without interfering with any of them. All text here is synthetic.

What the companion must never break

Everything below is a real control. The character follows the cursor across this page and is expected to sit over these elements without swallowing a single click or keystroke.

Clicks: 0

The companion is decorative and carries no text of any kind.

Scroll and resize

The companion is positioned in viewport coordinates, so scrolling the page under a stationary cursor must leave it exactly where it was. This region exists to scroll.

Hostile page styles

The document stylesheet above resets every element with all: revert. The companion draws inside its own closed shadow root and must keep its own colours, silhouettes and pointer-events regardless.
